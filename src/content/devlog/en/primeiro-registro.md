The devlog is where things go when they do not deserve a whole article but I would forget
them in two weeks. A problem, what I tried, what fixed it. No long introduction and no
closing life lesson.

On the technology side:

- why that process got slow and what fixed it
- the real cost of a choice that looked harmless
- the configuration trap that cost me an afternoon
- a new tool that earned its place in the habit, or did not

On the finance side:

- a yield, tax or financing calculation that did not come out the way I expected
- a headline number that changes face once you strip out the fees
- a dumb spreadsheet automation that saved an hour a month

The format is the same on both sides: short text, tags at the end, no decoration. The
first reader is me, six months from now.
