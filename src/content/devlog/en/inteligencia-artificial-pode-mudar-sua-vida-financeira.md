AI can be a huge help in managing your finances if you have at least a basic idea of what you're doing.

I've been telling all my friends for a while now that if you don't keep track of your cash flow, you'll end up frustrated at some point. Because that's the log that tells you whether you're doing well or poorly, whether you're improving or getting worse financially, and what you can or can't afford until the end of the month. And you don't need to be a spreadsheet or programming expert to do it: a simple list with dates, descriptions, amounts, and income or expenses already solves 99% of your problems.

Today, with a simple prompt, you can ask any model with good reasoning capabilities to analyze your cash flow, generate insights and dashboards, and answer a few questions. This can make all the difference in turning you from a person in debt, who owes the bank and other people, into someone who starts building wealth little by little and achieves their goals over time.
